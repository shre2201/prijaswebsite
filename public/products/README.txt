Product Images Folder
=====================
Place product images here. Recommended format: WebP or PNG, square aspect ratio (1:1), min 800x800px.

Naming convention:
  <product-slug>.png   e.g. neem-turmeric-face-wash.png
  <product-slug>-2.png (alternate angle)

These are referenced in the Product schema's `images` array field as "/products/<filename>".
